We zijn niet alleen

2026 · Koen van Seuren & Luca Bötzel · De Parade
Role: text & direction

A raging punk monologue about conspiracy thinkers. They turn up at family parties, at the bus stop, or they wait your table in a restaurant. One believes the earth is flat, another that a gang of aliens runs the world.

Luca Bötzel delivers the testimony of a man who knows for certain that they are out there. Bit by bit the extraterrestrial truth turns out to lie much closer to home: in loss, displacement and class. A look inside the conspiracy thinker's head, and under the bonnet of our society.

Played at the touring theatre festival De Parade in The Hague, Amsterdam and Utrecht (summer 2026), sold out every night from The Hague onwards. Reviewed by Han van Wieringen in De Groene Amsterdammer.
