Storm in a Glass of Water

2027 · Het Zuidelijk Toneel · De Parade · Short Summer Show · summer 2027
Role: text & direction

Disaster theatre for disaster tourists. Teun Donders plays a Dutch storm chaser who takes the audience along on the hunt for the storm that will finally wipe the country off the map. Why settle for a code yellow and a blown-over party tent when you could go under in a supercell?

A stormy half hour of satirical theatre: Het Zuidelijk Toneel's Short Summer Show for the touring festival De Parade, summer 2027.
