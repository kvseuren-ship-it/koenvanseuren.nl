Peer Gynt (speelt David Westera)

2022 · Antiklimax / Grand Theatre Groningen
Role: concept & text (with David Westera), direction

A tour de force of lies and megalomania. Antiklimax rewrote Ibsen's monumental play, with its more than sixty characters, into a monologue for one lonely liar: a twenty-something searching for his title role on the world stage. Who are you really, when you lie?

First made as a graduation production at the Toneelacademie Maastricht (2020); a new version premiered at Grand Theatre Groningen in 2022.
