On Hold

2022 · Finn Borath / Likeminds
Role: final direction

A visual performance by Finn Borath about boredom, futility and bullshit jobs: a man trapped in an office job that consists only of reading, stamping and sorting documents that nothing is ever done with. Shown at Likeminds bedankt!, Amsterdam-Noord.
