We Are The World, We Are The Brabant

2025 · Het Zuidelijk Toneel / Sarah Moeremans
Role: assistant director

An initiation ritual with amateur performers from Brabant. What makes someone a Brabander, and when do you belong? The Het Zuidelijk Toneel ensemble and local performers from each tour city turn the evening into a red-and-white-chequered Brabant party, with Schrobbelèr and sausage rolls on stage.
